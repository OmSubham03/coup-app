Set up your home table

  1. Choose Play poker now. The host chooses the starting buy-in, small blind, and whether to enable the big blind before creating the room.
  2. Choose display names and share the five-character code. Friends open Poker and join with that code, or enter a public room when the host enables discovery.
  3. The host starts with two to eight players. Watch the turn indicator and the bet shown beneath each seat before choosing an action.

Chips and buy-ins are game values, not payments or redeemable prizes. No download is needed. A saved game in the same tab reconnects before a new game link opens.

Build the best five-card hand

Each player receives two hidden hole cards. The shared board appears in stages: three cards on the flop, one on the turn, and one on the river. At showdown, make your strongest five-card hand from any combination of your two cards and the five community cards. You may use both hole cards, one, or neither.

Betting choices

  • Fold gives up the hand and chips already committed.
  • Check passes only when you owe no additional chips. Call matches the outstanding bet within your available stack.
  • Raise increases the bet. The controls show the legal minimum and maximum; the displayed amount is the total bet you are raising to, not just an extra increment.
  • All in commits your remaining stack. Do not treat it as a way to reset a losing hand.

If everyone else folds, the remaining player wins without needing a showdown. Otherwise the server compares hand strength and kickers; equally strong winning hands share the pot. Suits are not a tiebreaker.

Rankings and blinds

From highest to lowest: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card. An ace can be low in A-2-3-4-5 or high at the top of a straight.

The dealer button rotates between hands. With the big blind enabled, it is twice the small blind; disabling it is a TimeCrusher table option. In a two-player hand, the dealer posts the small blind. Check the room settings rather than assuming every table uses the same stakes.

The board can help both players

You hold ace of hearts and queen of clubs. The board is ace of clubs, queen of diamonds, seven of spades, three of clubs, and two of diamonds. Your best five cards make two pair: aces and queens, with a seven kicker.

A rival holding two sevens has three sevens using the board's seven. Three of a kind beats your two pair even though your pair ranks are higher. Count the complete hand category before deciding that a top pair or two pair must be ahead.

Make each decision about the current hand

  • Track how much a call costs relative to the pot, not just the size of your starting stack.
  • Re-evaluate after each new community card; a river can complete a straight or flush for an opponent.
  • A fold preserves chips for later hands. Money already in the pot is not a reason to keep calling.
  • Start with small virtual blinds while learning the controls, and agree with friends on when to end the session.
