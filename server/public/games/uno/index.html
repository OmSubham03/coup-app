Agree on the room rules first

  1. Select Play UNO now. The creator can enable draw-card stacking and Multi Skip before creating the room; both are optional house rules.
  2. Share the five-character code and join with display names. Friends do not need to reselect the creator's options when joining.
  3. The host starts with two to six players. Check the lobby's rule summary so everyone knows which version they are playing.

No download or account sign-in is needed. A public room can fill available seats. A saved game reconnects before a new link opens its setup.

Match, draw, and manage your last card

Each player starts with seven cards. Play one matching the current color or symbol, or a permitted Wild. You may choose to draw instead of playing a card already in your hand. After drawing, you may play that drawn card if legal, or end your turn.

Skip misses the next player. Reverse changes direction and acts as Skip with two players. Draw Two normally makes the next player draw two and miss a turn. A Wild sets the next color.

Wild Draw Four and calling UNO

Wild Draw Four is legal only when its player has no card matching the current color. The next player can challenge: an illegal play makes its actor draw four and lets the challenger continue; a legal play makes the challenger draw six and lose the turn.

When you reach one card, call UNO before the next player begins. If another player catches you first, draw two. A hand ends when someone empties their cards, after any final draw penalty is resolved.

The two optional house rules

  • Stacking: +2 may follow +2, +4 may follow +2 or +4, and a +2 may follow +4 only if it matches the color chosen after that +4. The next player accepts the accumulated draw total or adds a permitted draw card.
  • Multi Skip: the player about to be skipped may respond with another Skip of any color. Once a player cannot extend the chain, the game skips as many turns as there are Skips, starting with that player and following the current direction. In a two-player game those skipped turns can wrap around.

The hand winner scores opponents' remaining cards: numbers at face value, action cards at 20, and Wilds at 50. The first player to reach 500 points wins the match.

Check the color before stacking

Stacking is enabled. A player adds Wild Draw Four to a pending Draw Two and chooses green, bringing the accumulated penalty to six. You can extend it with another Wild Draw Four, or with a green Draw Two if you have one.

A red Draw Two cannot answer that green +4 in this house rule. If you accept instead, you draw the accumulated six and the penalty is resolved. Without stacking enabled, do not expect a Draw Two in your hand to cancel a pending draw penalty.

Watch the rule summary as well as the color

  • Save a flexible Wild for a hand with few color options, not merely because it looks powerful.
  • Call UNO as soon as the one-card opportunity appears; do not wait for someone else to notice.
  • Challenge a +4 based on evidence about the matching color, not just frustration about drawing.
  • Learn Multi Skip with friends before using it in a two-player match where the chain can wrap.
